## Student Loan Repayment Strategies

### Overview

- as a plan 2 student loan holder what is the optimal repayment strategy to maximise terminal wealth at the end of the loan?
- this repo builds a stochastic salary model to project future salaries
- these salary paths feed a loan model of which investment or repayment strategies can be injected
- the expectation and median values are taken across many paths

### Strategies

1. Minimum Repayment
2. Overpayment
3. Investment in an index fund
4. Investment in a covered call strategy
5. Partial overpayment and partial investment in strategies 3, 4
